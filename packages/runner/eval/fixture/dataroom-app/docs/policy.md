# Data policy

- **Audit log.** Audit entries are kept for 7 years and are never deleted, including when a person
  or a document is removed. They are the record of who shared what with whom.
- **Personal data.** A person's own data is theirs: when they leave, their documents, links and
  the views recorded against them are removed on request. What must be kept (the audit log) keeps
  no more about them than it needs.
- **Visitors.** We record that a link was opened, when, by which recipient email and with which
  browser (user agent). We do not record IP addresses or locations.
