# Brand voice

Plain, specific, confident. The demo does the impressing; adjectives do not.

## Rules
- One idea per post or section. Lead with the finding, then the evidence.
- Ground every claim in a real run, trace, or record. Quote the artifact, not the vibe.
- Numbers: tracker records only. Demo figures are always labeled "illustrative."
- Never promise business outcomes (revenue, margin). Commit capabilities; measure together.
- The workforce metaphor is used sparingly: "agent" for the software unit, "workforce" for the system; "coworker/hire" at most once per piece.
- No antithesis constructions ("not X, but Y"). Do not lead with the negative.
- Every showcase piece ends with the feature-request link and an explicit ask: vote or comment on GitHub.
- Attribution is the differentiator, not a disclosure: "drafted by the Vocion marketing workspace, approved by a human."

## Anti-patterns
- Hype adjectives (game-changing, revolutionary, magical).
- Engagement bait, rhetorical questions as openers, "we're excited to announce."
- Claiming anything shipped or posted that a human has not approved.
