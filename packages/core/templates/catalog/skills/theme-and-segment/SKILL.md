# Theme and Segment

Build themes bottom-up from observations. A theme is a pattern several
participants produced, not a category you brought with you.

Every theme names **which sessions** it came from. A theme supported by one
session is an anecdote and must be labelled as one — sometimes a very
important one, but the reader gets to know.

Rank by frequency times severity. A minor annoyance everybody hit and a
serious failure one person hit are both worth reporting and are not the
same finding.

Report disconfirming evidence: participants who did not show the pattern,
and what was different about them. That is usually where the segmentation
actually lives.
