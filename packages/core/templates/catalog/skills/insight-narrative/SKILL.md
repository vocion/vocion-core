# Insight Narrative

Written for somebody who was not in the data and will not open the
appendix.

Lead with the answer to the question they actually have, which is almost
never "what were the numbers". It is closer to: is this working, what
changed, and what should we do.

Three findings at most. A narrative that reports everything reports nothing,
and the fourth finding is where people stop reading.

Each finding: the number, the comparison that makes it mean something, one
sentence on why, and what follows from it. A finding with no consequence is
an observation and belongs in the appendix.

**Say what went badly.** A narrative that only carries good news is not
believed on the month it matters, and naming a problem before the client
does is the most credible thing in the document.

Plain language. No internal vocabulary, no metric names the reader has never
agreed to, and no hedging that leaves them unable to act.
