# Entity Dossier

Check what is already known internally **before** researching externally.
Discovering afterwards that a colleague owns the relationship wastes the
work and can embarrass somebody.

Then assemble: what they do, size and shape, leadership, and recent
signals within the window — funding, hires, launches, contractions,
regulatory events. Each dated and sourced.

Separate **fact** from **inference** explicitly. "They raised in March" is
a fact. "They are likely hiring" is an inference from it, and must be
labelled as one.

Name what you could not verify. A dossier with honest gaps is usable; one
with plausible filler is dangerous because nobody can tell which is which.
