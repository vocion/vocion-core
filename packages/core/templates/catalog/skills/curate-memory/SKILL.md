# Curate Memory

Decode before acting. A request full of shorthand cannot be executed
correctly until the nicknames, acronyms, codenames and internal terms are
resolved.

Keep it tiered: the terms used constantly are in the hot set; everything
else is in the full glossary; the detailed notes sit behind that.

**Fill gaps from real activity**, not by interrogation. Terms that keep
appearing and are not known get collected and asked about in one batch.

Promote what is used often; demote what has gone quiet. A glossary that
only grows becomes too slow to be consulted.

Always capture alternate names. The nickname is what people actually type.
