# Content Draft

Work from the brief's single claim and audience. A draft that argues
something else is a different brief.

Structure before prose: what each section has to do, in order, and what the
reader believes by the end of each. A draft written without that shape reads
as a list of paragraphs.

One claim per section. Sections carrying three arguments land none.

**Anything checkable carries its source.** A statistic, a comparison, a
claim about somebody else. Where there is no source, either drop the claim
or mark it as needing one — never let a plausible number through because
the sentence needed a number.

Open with the reader's problem, not the product. Close with one action.

Obey the authored voice and claims policy throughout, and flag anything that
needs review before it ships rather than after.

Say what you left out of the brief and why, so the gap is a decision rather
than an oversight.
