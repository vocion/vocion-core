# Fit Score

The profile is authored — dimensions, thresholds, disqualifiers. Apply it
as written.

One row per dimension: the criterion, what you found, the source, and
whether it passes. A dimension you could not verify says **not verified**,
never a guess and never an average.

Disqualifiers are checked first and are absolute. One disqualifier ends the
assessment regardless of how well everything else scores.

End with an overall verdict and one sentence of reasoning. Where too many
dimensions are unverified to support a verdict, say that instead — and say
which one, checked first, would move it most.
