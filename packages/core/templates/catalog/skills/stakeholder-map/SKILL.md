# Stakeholder Map

Per person: role, their stake in the outcome, their position as evidenced
rather than assumed, and when they were last engaged and by whom.

Distinguish decision-makers, influencers, users and blockers. They need
different things, and treating an influencer as a decision-maker is a
common and expensive error.

**Flag the gaps explicitly**: roles that ought to be involved and are
absent, people who have gone quiet, and dependence on a single relationship.
Coverage resting on one person is the single largest risk in most
relationships.

Mark anyone new, and anyone whose role has changed. A reorganisation on
their side invalidates the map faster than anything else.
