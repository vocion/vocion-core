# Onboarding Plan

**Before they start** is where onboarding succeeds or fails: access,
equipment, accounts, and a named person expecting them. Each with an owner
and a date that is not the start date.

Day one: who meets them, what they need to know to function, and one thing
they can complete. No system walkthrough marathon.

Week one: the people to meet and why, the context to absorb, and a first
piece of real work small enough to finish.

Then goals at thirty, sixty and ninety days, expressed as what they will be
able to do rather than what they will have read.

Name the person accountable for each item. Onboarding fails by diffusion of
responsibility more than anything else.
