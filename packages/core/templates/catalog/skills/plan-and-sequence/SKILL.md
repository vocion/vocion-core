# Plan and Sequence

Steps first, dates last. A date chosen before the sequence is a wish.

For each step: what it produces, who owns it, what must be true before it
starts, and how long it takes. Estimate in the org's authored unit.

Derive dates from the sequence and the estimates, then compare against any
externally fixed date. Where they disagree, **say so immediately** and name
the options: cut scope, add people, or move the date. Do not compress
estimates to make a plan fit.

Mark every step that depends on somebody outside the team. Those are where
plans actually break, and they need naming on day one rather than the week
they slip.
