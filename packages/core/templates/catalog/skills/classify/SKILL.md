# Classify

The taxonomy and the priority definitions are authored, not yours. Use
them exactly as written.

Read the whole item before classifying — context later in a thread often
changes the assessment. Classify by **root cause**, not by the symptom
described: somebody who cannot log in because of a bug is a bug, not an
access request.

Priority comes from impact times urgency against the written definitions.
Attach one line of justification naming which criterion was met. When
genuinely between two levels, take the higher one and say why — it is
cheaper to de-escalate than to miss a target.

Where the item does not fit the taxonomy, say so rather than forcing it
into the nearest box. A steady trickle of unfitting items is a signal the
taxonomy needs a new category.
