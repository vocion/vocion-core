# Citation Tracking

**Fix the prompt set first**, and keep it fixed. A question list that drifts
between runs produces a trend that is an artifact of the list.

Per question, capture: whether you are cited, in what position, what is
actually said about you, and who else is cited alongside. The last one is
the competitive read and is usually the most useful.

**Capture the answer text, not just the fact of a citation.** Being cited
inaccurately is worse than not being cited, and only the text shows it.

Record the date and the engine on every run. These outputs are
non-deterministic and change without notice, so an undated capture cannot
be compared to anything.

Report movement, not level. "Cited on 6 of 20, up from 3, and the three new
ones are all pricing questions" is the finding.

Where you are described wrongly, quote it exactly and trace what source it
most likely came from — that is where the fix goes.
