# Audience-tiered Update

Establish the state once — what is done, what is in flight, what is
blocked, what changed since last time — then render it per audience.

- **Executive.** Two or three paragraphs at most. Lead with the decision
  needed or the risk. Numbers against target. No mechanism.
- **Team.** What moved, who is blocked on whom, what lands next. Names and
  dates. Mechanism is welcome here.
- **External.** Only what is committed and shareable. No internal
  timelines, no roadmap, no names of people who did not agree to be named.

The same fact must not contradict itself between versions. If something is
amber for the team it is not green for the executive — the difference
between versions is depth and framing, never substance.
