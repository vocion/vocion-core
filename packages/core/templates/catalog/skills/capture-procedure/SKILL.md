# Capture Procedure

**If they just did it in this conversation, that transcript is the
specification.** Extract it rather than interviewing them again — and the
corrections they made along the way are the most valuable part, because
they encode judgement nobody would have thought to state.

Where it has to be described instead, ask them to walk through the last
real instance, concretely. Specifics beat description every time.

Capture: the trigger, the inputs and where they come from, the sequence,
the decision points, the output and who receives it, and how they know it
worked.

Note every place they hesitated, checked something, or said "it depends".
Those are the judgement points, and they become the gates.
