# Ad Variants

Work from the brief's single claim. Variants that argue different things are
not variants, they are separate ads, and testing them tells you nothing
about either.

**Respect the character limits exactly**, per field and per placement. A
variant that truncates in the wild was never tested — it was a different ad.
Count the characters and show the count.

Each variant differs in **one** named way: the angle, the proof, the call to
action, the specificity of the number. Say which axis each one moves, so a
result can be read.

Three to five is the useful range. More than that splits the volume until
nothing reaches significance.

Every claim obeys the authored claims policy. A variant that outperforms and
cannot be substantiated is worse than one that loses.

Flag anything that needs legal or brand review before it runs, rather than
letting it reach a platform and be pulled.
