# Catch Up

Ask for or infer the window, then answer one question: what is different
now from when they left?

Order strictly by what needs them:
1. **Needs a decision from you** — blocked on their answer, with how long
   it has been waiting.
2. **Happened, and you would want to know** — decisions taken in their
   absence, things that shipped, things that broke.
3. **Still running** — unchanged and fine.

Do not replay the timeline. Somebody back from a week away does not want
seven daily digests concatenated; they want the delta and the things now
pointing at them.
