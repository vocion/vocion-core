# Obligation Tracker

Extract obligations as they are created: what was promised, by whom, to
whom, by when, and the source with its exact reference.

Include the ones nobody diaries: notice periods, reporting commitments,
renewal windows, reporting deadlines, and anything triggered by an event
rather than a date.

Every obligation has a named individual owner. An obligation owned by a
function is an obligation owned by nobody.

Report by due date with enough lead time to act. An obligation reported the
week it is due has already failed for anything requiring preparation.

Flag obligations whose owner has left. That is the most common way a
commitment is quietly dropped.
