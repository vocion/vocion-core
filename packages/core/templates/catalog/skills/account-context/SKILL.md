# Account Context

Pull from everything connected and merge into one picture, organised by
topic rather than by system.

Cover: what the records say, the last few exchanges and what was committed
in them, documents that mention them, what recent conversations surfaced,
and what colleagues have said internally.

Internal discussion is useful and sensitive. Include what bears on the
relationship; exclude anything that would be damaging if it were forwarded,
because eventually something will be.

Every line cites its source and date. Lead with what changed most recently —
somebody asking for context usually wants to know what they missed.
