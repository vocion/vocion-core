# Knowledge Article

Title it as the reader would describe the problem, not as the system
describes it. That title is what search matches on.

Open with the symptom, so somebody confirms in one line they are in the
right place. Then who it affects and under what conditions.

Steps that are numbered, exact and complete, including how to confirm each
worked. No step may assume knowledge the reader has not been given.

Where there is a workaround and a fix, give the workaround first — the
reader is blocked now.

Say what to do if it does not work, and where to go next. An article with
no exit leaves somebody stuck with nowhere to turn.
