# Capture Context

**Read what can be read before asking.** Currency, country and fiscal year
end are usually available from a connected system, and asking for what is
already visible reads as not having looked.

Then establish, in one batch rather than one question at a time: what they
do and who for, the source of record per category where more than one
system is connected, how they want output delivered, and what actually
frustrates them.

The last one shapes everything. What somebody says is painful tells you
which definitions matter.

Record what is a stated fact and what is an assumption, so an assumption is
never later cited as something they said.
