# Campaign Plan

One objective, stated as a change in a number, with a baseline and a
target. A campaign with two objectives has none.

Audience defined by something observable, not by persona adjectives. Who
they are, where they are reachable, and what they currently believe.

Message as a single claim the campaign has to land, plus the evidence for
it. Every asset is a rendering of that claim; if an asset does not carry
it, it does not belong.

Channels chosen for where the audience is, with a stated budget split.
Calendar with dependencies — assets needed before the date they run, and
who produces them.

Success measured on the objective. Impressions are not the objective.
