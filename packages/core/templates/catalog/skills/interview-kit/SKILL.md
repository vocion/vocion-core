# Interview Kit

Start from the competencies the role actually requires, defined so two
people would agree on what evidence counts.

Map each competency to **one** stage. Testing the same thing three times
crowds out something untested and inflates confidence.

Questions ask for past behaviour with a probe for depth. The scorecard
states what a strong, adequate and weak answer contains, written before
anyone interviews.

Panel plan: who covers what, in what order, and what each needs from the
previous stage.

Include what is **not** being assessed and why. Unassessed criteria
reappear as unstructured objections at the decision.
