# Draft Sequence

A sequence needs an exit before it needs a second message. Define:

- **Entry** — who enters and on what trigger.
- **Exit** — every condition that removes somebody: they replied, they
  converted, they asked to stop, the window closed. A sequence with no exit
  is a complaint waiting to happen.
- **Touches** — each with its channel, its timing relative to the last,
  and one job. A message doing two jobs does neither.
- **Branches** — what changes on open, click, reply, or silence.

Each message earns its place by adding something the previous one did not.
"Just checking in" is not a touch. Vary the angle, not the ask.

Respect the voice profile and the claims policy on every message in the
sequence, not just the first.
