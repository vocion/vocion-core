# Channel Performance

**Name the attribution model before the numbers.** First touch, last touch
and multi-touch produce different winners from identical data, and a report
that does not say which is being used cannot be argued with.

Per channel: spend, the outcome on the campaign objective, cost per
outcome, and the change against the prior period.

Say which channels cannot be measured, rather than excluding them. An
unmeasurable channel excluded from the table reads as a channel with no
return.

**Name the channel that is not working**, including one somebody senior
likes. That is the entire reason the report exists.

Where volumes are too small to be meaningful, say so rather than reporting
a ratio built on four events.
