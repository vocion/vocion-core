# Review Pack

Open with what they got, in their terms, with evidence. Not activity
volume — outcomes against what they said they wanted.

Then what did not go well. A review that only reports success is not
believed and is not useful; naming a problem before they do is the single
most credible thing in the meeting.

Then what is next and what you need from them.

**Leave room.** A pack that fills the whole slot leaves no time for the
conversation that was the actual point of meeting.

Every figure cited to its source and dated. A number they can disprove in
the room costs more than it was ever going to earn.
