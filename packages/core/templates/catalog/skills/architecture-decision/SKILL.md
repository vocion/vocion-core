# Architecture Decision

Written for somebody arriving in two years wondering why.

- **Context** — the forces at play. Constraints, requirements, what was
  already true.
- **Options** — genuinely considered, each with what recommends it. An
  option list with one real candidate is a justification, not a decision.
- **Decision** — what was chosen, stated plainly.
- **Consequences** — what this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it
  makes expensive to reverse. Including the bad ones is what makes the
  record trustworthy.
- **Revisit when** — the condition that should reopen it.

Record the date and who decided. A decision with no owner cannot be
questioned properly.
