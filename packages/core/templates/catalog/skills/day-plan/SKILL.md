# Day Plan

Put the two together: what is scheduled, and what was promised for today
or is already late.

Then do the arithmetic honestly. Count the meeting hours, subtract from the
working day, and compare what is left against what has been committed. If
it does not fit, **say so** and say what to move. A plan that pretends
everything fits is the reason the day fails.

Flag meetings with no prep done where prep is needed, and commitments due
today with no time anywhere near them.

End with the three things that must happen today, in order.
