# Pattern Detect

Look across items rather than at them. A cluster in one area, several
reports since a change, the same question repeatedly, or a rise in one
category.

Individually low-priority items that keep arriving are a high-priority
pattern. That is precisely the case per-item triage cannot see, and the
reason this exists.

Escalate the pattern with its evidence: how many, over what window,
compared to what baseline, and what changed just before it started.

Distinguish a real rise from a reporting change. More reports after a
reporting change may mean better visibility rather than a worse problem —
say which you believe and why.
