# Session Notes

One observation per row: what the participant did or said, the quote or
timestamp behind it, and the task or question it occurred in.

**Separate observation from interpretation.** "Clicked back three times
then abandoned" is an observation. "Found the navigation confusing" is an
interpretation of it, and belongs in its own column so somebody else can
disagree with the reading while keeping the fact.

Capture what surprised you. The moments that did not fit the script are
where the useful findings usually are.

Keep participant identifiers consistent and pseudonymous, per the consent
and PII policy in force.
