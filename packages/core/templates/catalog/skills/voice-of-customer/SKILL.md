# Voice of Customer

Quote exactly. Trimming for length is fine and marked; tidying grammar or
sharpening a point is not — a cleaned-up quote is a paraphrase wearing
quotation marks.

Each quote carries who said it, in what context, when, and where it came
from. A quote with no date cannot be weighed.

Group by theme, and within a theme show the range rather than only the
quotes that agree. If three said one thing and one said the opposite,
showing all four is the finding.

Say how many sources were searched and how many quotes were found. Five
quotes out of five hundred conversations means something different from
five out of six.
