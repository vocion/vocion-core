# Change Control

A change record answers five questions and stands on its own for somebody
reading it in six months:

1. **What changes** — precisely, including what does not.
2. **Why now** — the driver, and the cost of not doing it.
3. **Impact** — on scope, dates, cost, and who else is affected.
4. **Rollback** — the specific steps to undo it, and the point after which
   undoing is no longer possible. A change with no rollback is a decision,
   and should be labelled one.
5. **Approval** — who must agree, per the authored path, and who did.

Draft it for approval. Recording a change as approved is never part of
proposing it.
