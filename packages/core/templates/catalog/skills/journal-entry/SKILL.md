# Journal Entry

Balance first. An entry that does not balance is not a draft, it is an
error.

Each line: account, debit or credit, amount, and the reason this account.
Against the authored chart of accounts — never invent an account.

**The support is part of the entry**, not an attachment to it. What
document, what calculation, what period it relates to, and who prepared it.
Written for somebody reviewing it a year from now with no memory of the
context.

Recurring entries state their schedule and their reversal, where they
reverse. Manual entries state why the transaction did not arrive through a
normal path — that reason is what an auditor samples for.
