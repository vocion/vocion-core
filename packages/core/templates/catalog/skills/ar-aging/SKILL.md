# Receivables Aging

Pull the aging from the source of record. Bucket by the authored bands and
report the total and each bucket, with the count.

**Check for recent payment before drafting anything.** A payment settled
and not yet applied is the fastest way to send a reminder to somebody who
already paid. Anything with a recent settlement is flagged for verification
and excluded from the drafts.

Score each account on its own history — consistently prompt, occasionally
late, persistently late — and match the tone to the score. One message per
account consolidating everything outstanding, never one per invoice.

Nothing sends without approval. Present the summary, then the drafts.
