# Write Spec

Open with the problem and the success measure, then:

- **Goals** — what this must achieve, in outcome terms.
- **Non-goals** — what it deliberately will not do. The most valuable
  section, and the most often skipped.
- **Requirements** — what must be true of the solution, not how to build
  it.
- **Acceptance criteria** — testable. Somebody must be able to determine
  pass or fail without asking the author.
- **Phasing** — what ships first and what it proves.
- **Open questions** — with an owner and a date needed by.

Where a decision has been made, record the reasoning and the alternatives
rejected. Six months later that is the only part anybody needs.
