# Statements

Produce against the authored chart of accounts and the fiscal calendar.
Never assume a calendar year.

Every statement shows the current period, the comparative period, and the
variance in both absolute and percentage terms.

**Check the ties before presenting**: the result flows to equity, the cash
movement reconciles to the balance movement, and the sub-totals sum to the
totals. Say the ties were checked.

Where a figure is provisional — an estimate, an unclosed account, an
unreconciled balance — mark it on the face of the statement, not in a note
nobody reads.
