# Announcement

Lead with what is changing and when. Not background, not the reasoning —
the change.

Say who is affected, so a reader knows in one line whether to keep reading.

**State what action anybody must take, and by when.** An announcement
requiring action that buries it is an announcement whose action will not be
taken.

Then the reasoning, honestly. Where the change is unwelcome, say so
directly; spin is recognised and costs more than the bad news.

Say where questions go and who answers them. Ship the internal note
separately — anticipated objections, what is not being said publicly, and
who to escalate to — never in the announcement itself.
