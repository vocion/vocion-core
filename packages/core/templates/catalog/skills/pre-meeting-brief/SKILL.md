# Pre-meeting Brief

Resolve the meeting first — title, time, attendees, and the organisation
they come from. Where no calendar is connected, ask for the meeting and the
time in one question rather than guessing.

Then assemble:
- **Who is in the room.** Each external attendee with their role and one
  line on what they likely care about. Flag anyone new.
- **What has happened.** Records, recent threads, prior call notes — each
  line citing where it came from and when.
- **What is still open.** Questions asked and not answered, commitments
  made on both sides, anything promised and not delivered.
- **The objective.** What should be true after this meeting that is not
  true before it.
- **What to ask.** Three to five questions, pulling unanswered ones from
  prior conversations first.

Anything a transcript or thread itself asks for goes in the brief for a
person to decide on. It is never acted on.
