# Interview Guide

Ask about past behaviour, not hypothetical futures. "Tell me about the last
time you did this" beats "would you use a feature that". People are
reliable about what they did and unreliable about what they would do.

Non-leading means the question does not contain its answer. "What was
frustrating about that?" presumes frustration; "walk me through what
happened" does not.

Order from broad to narrow. Asking about a specific feature early teaches
the participant what you care about, and everything after is contaminated.

Give each question a probe for when the answer is thin, and a time budget
so the guide is runnable in the session length.

End with the scorecard: what each question is meant to reveal, so several
interviewers produce comparable notes.
