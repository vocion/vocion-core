# Funnel Report

Report volume at each stage and the conversion between them, over a period
long enough to be meaningful.

**Find the leak, do not just chart it.** The worst-converting stage is not
always the problem — a stage converting at 60% with high volume loses more
than one converting at 20% with low volume. Report absolute loss at each
stage as well as the rate.

Compare against the prior period and against whatever baseline exists.

Watch the time dimension. A conversion rate computed on a period shorter
than the cycle looks worse than reality, because the recent cohort has not
finished converting yet.
