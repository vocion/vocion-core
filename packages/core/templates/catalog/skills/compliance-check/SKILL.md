# Compliance Check

Establish what the action actually involves before assessing it — what data,
whose, where it goes, who can see it, how long it is kept.

Then: which rules apply and why, what each requires, whether the proposal
meets it, and what is needed to close any gap.

List the approvals required, per the authored matrix, with who grants each.

**Separate what is required from what is advisable.** Mixing them means
requirements get negotiated like preferences.

Where applicability is genuinely unclear, say so and escalate. Deciding a
rule does not apply is itself a decision that needs the right owner.
