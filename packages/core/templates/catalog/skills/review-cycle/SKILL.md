# Review Cycle

The rubric is written and shared **before** the cycle opens. A rubric
revealed afterwards is not a rubric.

Self-assessment asks for evidence against the same dimensions the manager
uses. Different forms for the two sides produce an unproductive meeting.

Manager reviews cite specific evidence across the whole period. Guard
against recency — the last six weeks should not dominate a year.

Calibration compares ratings across managers for consistency of standard,
not to fit a distribution. Where two managers rate similar evidence
differently, that is the conversation.

Flag reviews with no specifics, ratings that changed in calibration without
a stated reason, and anybody whose reviewer has changed mid-period.
