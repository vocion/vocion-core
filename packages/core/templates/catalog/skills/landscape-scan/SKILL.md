# Landscape Scan

Name the comparison set and why those and not others — including the
do-nothing option, which is usually the real competitor.

Pick the axis before gathering. Comparing everybody on everything produces
a table nobody reads. Two or three dimensions that actually decide
something.

Per competitor: what they claim, what they demonstrably do, and the
distance between the two. Cite where each claim came from and when — a
positioning statement from two years ago is history, not intelligence.

End on the gap: what is true, useful, and currently unclaimed. That is the
output somebody can act on; the table is supporting material.
