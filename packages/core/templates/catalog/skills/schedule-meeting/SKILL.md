# Schedule Meeting

Check real availability first — both sides where you can see both, and the
working hours and time zone of whoever is being invited. A proposal in
somebody's evening is a proposal that gets declined.

Offer two or three times, never an open question. "When works?" starts
another round of messages, which is the thing this exists to avoid.

State the time zone on every proposed time, always, including when you
believe you share one.

The invitation carries a purpose, the expected length, and who else is
coming. An invitation with none of those is the one that gets declined
later by somebody who cannot tell what it is for.

**Show it before it sends.** Recipients, time, and body, exactly as they
will go out.

On a reschedule, propose from the same availability check rather than
shifting by an arbitrary amount, and confirm the move to everybody on the
invitation rather than only the person who asked.
