# Survey Design

Every question earns its place by mapping to a decision. Cut anything that
would be interesting but not actionable — length costs completion, and
completion costs representativeness.

One idea per question. A double-barrelled question produces an
uninterpretable answer.

State the scale and stay on it. Mixing five-point and seven-point scales in
one instrument makes responses incomparable.

**Before it ships**, check: leading wording, missing options that force a
false answer, an ordering that primes later questions, and whether the
sampling frame can reach the people whose answers would disconfirm the
hypothesis.

Say what response rate would make the results meaningful, before collecting
any.
