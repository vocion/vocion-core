# Content Calendar

Every item: what, channel, date, owner, and which campaign objective it
serves. Anything serving no objective comes out.

Schedule backwards from the publish date through review, production and
brief — and check the first date is not already in the past. Most calendars
fail at the brief, not at publication.

Test against capacity. A calendar that assumes everybody writes three
pieces a week when they historically write one is fiction.

Leave deliberate gaps for reactive work. A fully committed calendar cannot
respond to anything, and something always happens.
