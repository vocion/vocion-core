# Creative Brief

One page. A brief that needs a meeting to explain has not done its job.

- **The job** — what this asset must make somebody do or believe.
- **Audience** — who, and what they already think.
- **The single claim** — one sentence. Not three.
- **Evidence** — what supports the claim, and what may not be claimed
  without support.
- **Constraints** — format, length, channel, legal or brand boundaries.
- **What good looks like** — a reference, or the test it must pass.

State what is deliberately out of scope. Most rework comes from a brief
that was silent rather than one that was wrong.
