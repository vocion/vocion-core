# Whitespace Map

Start from what they actually have — from the records, not from memory.

Map against the full set of what exists. Per gap: is there evidence of a
need, has it been discussed before and what happened, and what would have
to be true for it to make sense now.

**A gap is not an opportunity.** Something they do not have and do not need
is correct, and listing it as whitespace produces a map nobody trusts. Mark
those as not applicable and say why.

Rank by evidence of need, not by size. And note what they have that they
are not using — that is usually more urgent than anything they do not have.
