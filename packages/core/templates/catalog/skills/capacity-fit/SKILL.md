# Capacity Fit

Start from real availability, not headcount. For each person: working days
in the window, minus leave, minus recurring meetings, minus a stated
allowance for interruption. What is left is capacity.

Then total the planned work in the same unit and compare.

**Report the gap in both directions.** Over-committed is the obvious
failure; materially under-committed usually means the estimates are
padded or work is missing from the plan, and both are worth knowing.

Never balance the books by shrinking estimates. If the work does not fit,
the output is a list of what to cut or move, ordered by what costs least.
