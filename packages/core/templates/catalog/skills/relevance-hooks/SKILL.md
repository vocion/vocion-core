# Relevance Hooks

A hook is specific, sourced, recent, and connected to something the reader
can actually help with. If any of the four is missing it is not a hook.

"They are growing" fails all four. "They posted eleven operations roles
last month and their new operations lead spoke about manual reconciliation
at a conference in March" passes.

Two or three hooks, each one sentence, each carrying its source and date so
somebody can check it before using it.

Where the research surfaced nothing specific, say so. A generic opener sent
as though it were researched is worse than an honest one, and the sender
should get to make that choice knowingly.
