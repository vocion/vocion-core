# Post-incident Review

Timeline first, from evidence: detection, escalation, mitigation,
resolution, each with a timestamp and a source. Include what people
believed at each point — most of the learning is in the gap between what
was true and what was known.

**Contributing factors, not a root cause.** Incidents are plural. Look at
the change, the monitoring, the escalation path, and the conditions that
made the failure possible.

Blameless means describing decisions in terms of the information available
at the time. "The runbook did not cover this case" — not "they missed it".

Actions must be specific, owned, and dated. "Improve monitoring" is not an
action. Include at least one that would have shortened detection, because
that is usually where the time went.
