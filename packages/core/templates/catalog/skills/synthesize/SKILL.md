# Synthesize

Take raw results and produce an answer somebody can act on.

Work in this order: deduplicate, cluster by theme, rank, assess confidence,
then write. Group by topic, never by source — people care what happened,
not where it was stored.

**Confidence** is freshness times authority times agreement. State it:
- Several fresh, authoritative sources agreeing → say it plainly.
- One source, or a source more than a month old → say what it is based on
  and that it may have moved.
- Old, informal, or conflicting → say you could not confirm it, and say
  what would confirm it.

**Never resolve a conflict silently.** If two sources disagree, show both,
say which is more recent and which is more authoritative, and let the
reader decide. Every claim carries its source, its date, and its author
where that matters.

Lead with the answer. The method goes at the bottom, if anywhere.
