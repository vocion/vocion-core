# Brief Pack

Written for a reader who was not in the conversation and cannot ask
follow-up questions.

Open with the answer or the recommendation. Then the evidence, organised by
theme. Then the gaps.

Every claim carries its source inline. A reader must be able to check any
individual line without reading the whole thing.

A **confidence line** near the top: how much of this is verified, how much
is inference, and what would most change the conclusion if it turned out
differently. That is the sentence that makes a brief safe to act on.
