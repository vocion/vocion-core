# Voice Check

The voice profile, the style guide and the claims policy are authored. Check
against them, not against taste.

Per finding: the exact text, which rule it departs from, why it matters,
and **the specific replacement**. Flagging without rewriting leaves the
work undone.

Severity matters: an unsupported claim about a competitor and an
inconsistent capitalisation are not the same finding and must not sit in
one undifferentiated list.

Check especially for: claims with no support, terminology the guide
replaced, borrowed phrasing that is not the author's, and anything
committing the organisation that the author cannot commit.

Say what is already right. A check returning only faults gets ignored.
