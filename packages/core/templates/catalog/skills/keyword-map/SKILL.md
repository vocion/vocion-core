# Keyword Map

Start from questions, not from volume. A high-volume term nobody in your
audience asks is a trap, and the long question with low volume is often the
one that converts.

Per question: the intent behind it, the page that should own it, and whether
that page exists.

Two findings come out of the map and both are more useful than the map:

- **Cannibalisation** — several pages competing for one question. Say which
  should own it and what happens to the others: merge, redirect, or
  re-point at a different question.
- **Gaps** — a question with real intent and no page. That is the content
  brief, and it should be handed over as one.

One page owns one question. A page trying to own several answers none of
them well enough to be chosen.

Say which questions you deliberately are not chasing and why. An unbounded
map gets abandoned.
