# Signal Watch

Report only events inside the stated window, each with its date and its
source. An undated signal is not a signal.

Categories worth watching: funding and financial events, leadership
changes, product launches, expansion or contraction, partnerships, legal
and regulatory events, and public statements by named executives.

Say what each signal **implies** for the reader, separately from the fact
itself and labelled as inference. A new operations lead implies a review of
existing suppliers; that is a reasonable inference and it is not a fact.

Silence is reportable. No signals in the window means either nothing
happened or nothing is visible, and those differ — say which you believe
and why.
