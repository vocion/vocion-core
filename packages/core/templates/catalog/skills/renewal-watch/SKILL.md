# Renewal Watch

Report by date order: what falls due in the window, its value, its current
health, and who owns it.

**Notice dates bind before renewal dates.** A relationship renewing in six
months with a ninety-day notice requirement has three months, and reporting
the renewal date alone hides that. Flag the notice date as the real one.

Flag the standard risks: health declined, champion departed, usage down,
issues unresolved, no contact in the period, or a date that has already
moved once.

For anything at risk, say what would need to happen and by when. A watch
list without that is a list of dates.
