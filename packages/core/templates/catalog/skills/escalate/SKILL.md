# Escalate

A good escalation is a complete handover. Six parts:

1. **One line** — what is wrong and for whom.
2. **Impact** — who is affected, how many, what is blocked, since when.
3. **History** — two or three sentences of how it got here.
4. **What has been tried** — including what was ruled out, so nobody
   repeats it.
5. **What is needed** — a specific decision, action or person. Not "help".
6. **By when**, and what happens if that passes.

Urgency comes from the authored thresholds, not from how the requester
phrased it. Escalating everything is the fastest way to make escalation
mean nothing.
