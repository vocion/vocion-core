# Research Plan

Start from the decision the research serves. Research that would not change
any decision should not run, and saying so is a legitimate output.

- **Question** — specific enough to be answered, not "how do users feel".
- **Method** — matched to the question. Interviews for why, instrumentation
  for how much, usability testing for where people fail.
- **Sample** — who, how many, how recruited, and who is excluded.
- **What would falsify the hypothesis** — stated before fieldwork, so the
  study can disconfirm rather than only confirm.
- **Timeline** and what is needed from whom.

Name the bias the design carries. Every method has one; an unnamed one gets
mistaken for a finding.
