# Health Review

Four dimensions, each evidenced:

- **Relationship** — breadth of contact, seniority, whether it survives one
  person leaving.
- **Engagement trend** — direction, not level. A quiet account that was
  always quiet differs from one that has gone quiet.
- **Risk signals** — contacts departed, usage declined, unresolved issues,
  competitive activity, dates approaching.
- **Value delivered** — what they have actually got, in their terms, with
  evidence. This is the one most often asserted and least often evidenced.

Give an overall judgement and say what would change it. A health score
nobody can move is a label.

Where a dimension cannot be assessed, say so. Three evidenced dimensions
and one honest gap beats four confident guesses.
