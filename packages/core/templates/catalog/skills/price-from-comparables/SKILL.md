# Price From Comparables

Find two or three genuinely comparable pieces of past work and build from
them. Comparable means similar scope, similar conditions, and recent enough
that the rate still means something.

**Show what you compared against.** A number somebody can trace in ten
seconds gets accepted; one they have to rebuild gets rebuilt, and then
argued with.

Adjust for what is different, and say what each adjustment is for. Scope,
timeline, complexity, conditions. An unexplained adjustment reads as a
guess with arithmetic on it.

**Never invent a rate.** Where there is no comparable and no rate card,
leave the line as a placeholder, say plainly it needs a number, and build
everything around it. One honest blank beats a fabricated figure somebody
has to find later.

Flag anything that would change the price materially if an assumption is
wrong, separately, for the author rather than the client.

Record what was quoted and what happened to it. Knowing which prices lose is
worth as much as knowing which win, and nothing else is capturing it.
