# Target Watch

Measure against the authored targets. Report time in current state and time
since first contact — they differ, and the second is what the waiting
person experiences.

Flag **before** the breach, with enough margin to act. A report of what
already breached is history.

Apply the bump triggers without being asked: waiting beyond target, several
reports of the same thing, an explicit escalation, a workaround that has
stopped working, or scope that has grown.

Report the queue's shape as well as individual items — how many are near
target, the oldest item, and whether the trend is improving. One breach is
an incident; a trend is a capacity problem.
