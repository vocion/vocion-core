# Visibility Audit

Start from the questions the audience actually asks, not from keyword
volume. Visibility on a question nobody asks is worth nothing.

Check presence and position for each, and what the current answer says —
including where a competitor or a stale page is answering on your behalf.

Group findings into **quick wins** and **structural work**, with an effort
estimate for each. An undifferentiated list of forty issues gets nothing
done.

Per fix: what to change, where, and what improvement to expect. Be honest
where the expected improvement is small — several small fixes may still be
worth less than one structural change.
