# Usability Findings

Severity is about consequence, not annoyance: did they fail the task, work
around it, or merely grumble. Use the authored bands.

Per finding: where it happened, what the participant was trying to do, what
they did instead, how many of them hit it, and the quote or clip that shows
it.

Propose a fix that is specific enough to act on and stops short of
designing the solution — the finding is the evidence, and the design is
somebody else's decision informed by it.

Report what worked as well. A list containing only failures gives a false
picture and gets the whole report discounted.
