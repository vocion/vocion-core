# Critique

Say what stage the work is at and review accordingly. Polish feedback on an
exploration wastes everybody's time.

Three passes, kept separate:
- **Usability** — can somebody complete the task, are states handled, what
  happens when it is empty, slow, or wrong.
- **Hierarchy** — does the most important thing read first, is the primary
  action obvious.
- **Consistency** — against the system, not against taste. Cite the
  component or token it departs from.

Per finding: what, where, why it matters, severity, and a specific
suggestion. "Feels cluttered" is not a finding.

Say what works and why. A critique that only lists problems gets discounted
whole.
