# Assess Risk

Per risk: what could happen, what would follow, how likely, and against
what evidence. Rate on the authored scales — an ad hoc scale cannot be
compared across risks and defeats the register.

**Mitigation with an owner and a date**, and the residual rating after it.
A mitigation that does not change the rating is not a mitigation.

Apply the authored escalation criteria mechanically. Escalation by
threshold rather than by judgement is the whole point; a threshold applied
inconsistently is not a threshold.

Record risks that have been accepted, with who accepted them and when. An
accepted risk that nobody can point to an acceptance for is an unmanaged
one.
