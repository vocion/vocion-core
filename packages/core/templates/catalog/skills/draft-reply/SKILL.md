# Draft Reply

Read the situation before writing: what kind of message this is, how long
they have waited, how senior they are, how technical, and what state the
relationship is in.

Structure: acknowledge what they said, deliver the substance, state the
next steps with who does what by when, close.

Length follows the channel. A chat reply is a few sentences; a formal
letter is not a chat reply padded out.

**Internal notes ship beside the draft, never inside it**: why this
approach, anything to verify before sending, what to be careful of, and
whether somebody else should see it first.

Never commit beyond what is authorised. Where the honest answer requires a
commitment you cannot make, say so in the notes rather than softening the
draft into vagueness.
