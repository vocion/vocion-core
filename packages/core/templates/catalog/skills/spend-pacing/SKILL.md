# Spend Pacing

Report three numbers together or the answer is unreadable: spent to date,
the budget, and the share of the flight elapsed.

Pacing is the ratio between the last two. Report it as a percentage of
expected, so 100% is on plan and the direction of any miss is obvious.

**Over- and under-pacing are different problems and get named separately.**
Over-pacing ends the flight early and the shortfall lands on somebody's
quarter. Under-pacing returns money nobody planned to return, and is the one
that goes unnoticed because nothing breaks.

Flag early enough to act. A pacing report delivered in the last few days of
a flight is a post-mortem.

Project to the end date on current rate and say what lands there. Where the
rate changed mid-flight, say when and by how much rather than averaging
across the change.

Never spend, pause or reallocate. This reads and reports; the change is
somebody's decision.
