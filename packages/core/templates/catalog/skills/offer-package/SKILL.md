# Offer Package

Components against the authored bands, with the placement reasoned from the
assessment and comparable internal positions. A placement that cannot be
explained internally creates a problem later.

Check internal consistency **before** the offer goes out. An offer that
places somebody above existing people at the same level is a decision
somebody should make deliberately.

The letter states every component, what is guaranteed and what is
conditional, and the conditions on the offer itself.

Negotiation guidance separately, for the hiring manager only: what has room
and what does not, what to do on each likely counter, and the point at
which to stop. Never inside the letter.
