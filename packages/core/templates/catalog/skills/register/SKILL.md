# Register

Record the phrases **they actually use**, not the canonical name. Somebody
who says "the Monday numbers" will never type the formal title, and a
definition nobody can reach is a definition nobody uses.

Include the near misses and the ambiguous ones, and say which wins. Two
definitions competing for one phrase is a routing bug that surfaces as
"it did the wrong thing".

Where it recurs, offer to schedule it. Scheduling is what turns something
they could run into something that happens without them, which is what
they wanted.

For anything scheduled: say what it will do unattended, and confirm that
anything consequential stays a proposal.
