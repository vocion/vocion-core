# Mutual Action Plan

Work backwards from the decision date and the steps that must precede it,
including the ones on their side that are easy to forget — internal
approvals, security review, procurement, legal.

Each step: what, who on both sides, by when, and what it unblocks.

Written to be shared. No internal pricing thinking, no risk commentary, no
language you would not want read aloud in their office.

**The value is agreement, not the document.** A plan they have not agreed to
is a wish list. Note which steps they have confirmed and which are still
assumed, and keep that distinction visible.
