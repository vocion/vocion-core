# Triage Agreement

Classify against the authored bands on arrival:

- **Standard** — matches the expected form, no unusual terms. Proceeds
  without specialist review.
- **Review** — departures within known territory. Needs a look, not an
  escalation.
- **Full review** — unusual terms, material exposure, or a category the
  bands do not cover.

Screen specifically for the terms that decide the band: unusual obligations,
terms extending beyond the agreement's own life, anything binding a party
not present, and missing standard protections.

**Say what you checked.** A classification with no visible basis has to be
redone by whoever receives it, which removes the whole benefit.

When between two bands, take the higher one.
