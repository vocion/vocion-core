# Account Plan

Current state grounded in evidence: what they have, what it costs them,
what state the relationship is actually in, and when somebody last spoke to
whom.

Their goals, stated in their words with the source. An account plan built
on inferred goals is a plan for an imagined organisation.

Then: coverage against the stakeholder map, the opportunity map with what
would have to be true for each, and the risks with what would mitigate
them.

Actions are dated and owned, and there should be few. A plan with twenty
actions has none.

Say when the plan was last refreshed and against what. A stale plan
presented as current is worse than no plan.
