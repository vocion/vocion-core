# Roadmap View

Three horizons with honest confidence: **now** is committed, **next** is
intended, **later** is directional. Presenting all three with equal
certainty is what destroys roadmap credibility.

Per item: the problem it addresses, not the feature name. A roadmap of
feature names cannot be reprioritised because nobody can compare them.

**What changed since last time** is the most-read section. What moved in,
what moved out, what slipped, and why in one line each. A roadmap that
silently changes teaches people not to read it.

Say what is deliberately absent and frequently asked for. Answering that
once in the document saves answering it repeatedly.
