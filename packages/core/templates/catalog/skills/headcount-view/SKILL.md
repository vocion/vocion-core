# Headcount View

Current shape by team, level and function, against plan where one exists.

Span of control per manager, flagging both extremes. Too wide means nobody
is being managed; too narrow usually means a layer that exists for title
reasons.

Attrition by team and tenure band, with the trend rather than the point
figure. Say which departures were regretted where that is recorded.

**Name the single points of failure** — functions with one person, and
roles where a departure would block work with no cover. That is the most
actionable thing in the view and the least often reported.

Aggregate anywhere a figure could identify an individual.
