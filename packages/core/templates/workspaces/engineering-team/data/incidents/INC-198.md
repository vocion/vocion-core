## Symptom

Bulk imports over roughly 10,000 rows failed with a statement timeout
and returned nothing to the caller.

## Impact

Six accounts, twelve failed imports. Workaround was to split the file.

## Timeline

- 15:31Z — customer report.
- 16:10Z — reproduced at 12,000 rows; matched PLAT-3350.
- 18:05Z — closed with the workaround documented.

## Follow-up

Fixed by PR-1830, which batches at 500 rows and returns a per-batch
failure report. Documentation for the new `failed_rows` field in the
response is still outstanding.
