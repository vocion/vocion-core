# Service level commitments

| Plan | First response | Resolution (P2 and above) | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4 business hours | 3 business days | 99.5% monthly |
| Enterprise | 1 business hour | 2 business days | 99.5% monthly |

Business hours are 07:00–19:00 Eastern, Monday to Friday.

Priority definitions:

- **P1** — production is down and there is no workaround.
- **P2** — a workflow is blocked but a workaround exists.
- **P3** — degraded or confusing; the customer can still work.
- **P4** — a question or a request.

Attainment is measured on first response, not on resolution, because
first response is the number a customer feels. The desk's internal
target is 90% of tickets answered inside the plan's first-response
window.
