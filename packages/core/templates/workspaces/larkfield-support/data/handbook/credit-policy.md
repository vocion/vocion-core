# Credit and refund policy

**Downtime credit.** When monthly availability falls below 99.5%, the
account is owed the pro-rated monthly fee for the hours below the
commitment. This is an entitlement, not a favour — it does not need a
goodwill justification, but it still needs a human to approve the
amount.

**Goodwill credit.** Discretionary, capped at 10% of the monthly fee.
Up to 20% with a director's approval. One goodwill credit per account
per quarter.

**Refunds.** A refund of an already-paid invoice is Finance's decision.
Support routes the request; support does not size it.

**What support may never do without an approval.** Quote an amount to a
customer, commit to a credit in a reply, apply a credit to an account
record, or promise a fix date. Every one of those goes through the
`credit-approval` workflow or the reply approve gate.

**What a reviewer checks.** The clause the amount comes from, what the
customer was already told, the cheaper alternative that was considered,
and whether this account has already had a goodwill credit this quarter.
