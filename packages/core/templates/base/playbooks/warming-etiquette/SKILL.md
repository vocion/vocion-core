# Warming Etiquette

Relationship warming runs on warmth that is earned, one personal note at a
time. These rules apply to every follow-up, re-warming, and referral ask.

## Cadence

- One touch at a time. Never queue a second note before the first is
  answered or clearly stale.
- After an event or a first meeting, the follow-up is owed within the SLA
  the mission sets (default 4 days). After that it is late; say so.
- Re-warm a cold relationship (no touch in 90+ days) before ANY ask. The
  re-warming note carries no ask at all.

## Asks

- At most one ask per note, and only when warmth supports it.
- An intro request names the specific person or company and gives the
  connector an easy out.
- A touch with no ask (useful link, genuine congratulations, a relevant
  observation) is often the strongest move.

## Never

- Never fake familiarity or manufacture a hook that is not in the record.
- Never follow up more than twice on an unanswered ask; park it instead.
- Never make the recipient do work to figure out what is being asked.
