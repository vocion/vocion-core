# Win/Loss & Voice Signal

Capture the voice of the market from recent conversations (mail, chat, call
notes, CRM). Surface patterns the team can act on.

Return:

1. **Objections heard**: recurring pushback, with a verbatim quote each.
2. **Buyer language**: the exact words prospects use for their problem (feed
   messaging and SEO), not internal jargon.
3. **Proof points landed**: what evidence actually moved a deal forward.
4. **Product / delivery signals**: feature asks, friction, churn risk.
5. **This week's one thing**: the single insight worth acting on now.

Quote sources verbatim. Separate what was said from your interpretation.
