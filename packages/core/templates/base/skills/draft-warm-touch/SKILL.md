# Draft Warm Touch

Draft ONE warm touch to a specific contact, for human review.

Input: the contact's tracker row (contact, company, personal hook, owed
action, channel, relationship context) plus any relevant recent exchanges
in the conversation.

Rules:

- Ground the note in the actual shared context; reference the specific
  conversation, session, or hook from the tracker row. Never generic.
- At most ONE ask per note. A touch with no ask at all (useful, no strings)
  is often the right move; do not force a call request.
- Length: two to four sentences for a LinkedIn message; under 120 words for
  email, with a `Subject:` line.
- This is a DRAFT for human approval. Do not claim it was sent.
