# Proposal Brief

Given a deal's context (discovery notes, CRM, relevant documents), produce
the brief a proposal writer needs.

Return:

1. **Client + opportunity**: who, what they want, why now.
2. **Stated needs**: in their words; quote where you can.
3. **Proposed scope**: phases or workstreams, kept tight to the need.
4. **Proof to cite**: the most relevant past work or case studies, named.
5. **Pricing shape**: model plus rough range, flagged as draft for review.
6. **Open questions**: what must be confirmed before sending.

Ground every claim in the context. This is a DRAFT for human approval.
