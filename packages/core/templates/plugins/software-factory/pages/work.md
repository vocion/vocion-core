What we owe, what is running, what is stopped on you, and what just landed.
Four lanes, in that order. A row is one outcome; opening it opens that
outcome end to end, with its decisions, tasks, runs, checks, pull request and
cost.

**Waiting on you** is not a state anything stores. It is derived: an outcome
whose recommendation nobody has decided is not moving, whatever its state
says, and the heading carries the minutes those decisions are estimated to
take. The decisions themselves are still taken in
[Review](/dashboard/inbox), one batch at a time, never here.

**Next is ordered**, and only work whose reason was recorded is ranked. A
request with no recorded reason cannot be argued to be the second most
important thing we do, so it queues unnumbered and the heading says how many
are in that state. That is the one place this page reports the gap; it does
not repeat it in a column.

Probes, smoke tests and end-to-end records are not shown. They are real
history and they belong in [Activity](/dashboard/p/activity), with the
finished work that has scrolled past the cap.
