The outcome view. Each row is a product a person answers for; each number on
it is either **read from the record** (stage, our price, the incumbent's dated
list price, the accountable person) or **agent-maintained**, recomputed each
morning by the lead's `keep-the-board-honest` tick from the requests and
releases underneath and dated in the last column. A counter older than a day
is stale, and the board says so rather than showing yesterday's figure as
today's. Revenue is empty until a verified source exists; an empty cell is
honest, a zero is a claim.

**Shipped this month** and **Last shipped** are the per-product read of what
used to be its own Changelog page. The announcement line for each individual
release, in the words the public reads, is on the release record and on
[Releases](/dashboard/p/releases), under Activity.

What is still owed is on [Work](/dashboard/p/work); what it has cost is on
[Performance](/dashboard/p/performance).
