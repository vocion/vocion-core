The outcome view. Each row is a product a person answers for; each number on
it is either **read from the record** (stage, price, the incumbent's dated
list price) or **agent-maintained** — recomputed each morning by the lead's
`keep-the-board-honest` tick from the requests and releases underneath, and
dated in the last column. A counter older than a day is stale, and the board
says so rather than showing yesterday's figure as today's. Revenue is empty
until a verified source exists; an empty cell is honest, a zero is a claim.
The evidence for every figure is one section down, under **Software factory**.
