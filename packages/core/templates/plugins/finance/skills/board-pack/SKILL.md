# Board Pack

## Read before writing

- **The last packs.** Search the filed documents for the previous board
  packs, newest first, and read the most recent one whole: its sections, the
  title of every slide or page, and which reports recur every quarter.
  Weight recent packs over old ones.
- **The last board meeting.** Its transcript or notes: what was decided,
  what was asked for, and what was left open.
- **The numbers.** `finance_report` for the quarter and the comparative
  period: P&L, balance sheet, and by class or customer where the last pack
  broke figures down that way.

## Write

Follow the last pack's structure. A recurring report is rebuilt with this
quarter's figures. A decision from the last meeting gets its own line: what
was decided, what happened since, and whether the result bears it out. A
question the board asked is answered or marked as still open.

Every figure ties to a `finance_report` result for a stated period. Explain
each material swing with its drivers. Anything you cannot ground (a
headcount, a pipeline figure, a forecast nobody gave you) is a marked
placeholder, never a number you made up.

Render it as a document (`render_document`), verify it renders, and give
the person the link. It is a draft for the person who presents it; it goes
to nobody until they send it.
