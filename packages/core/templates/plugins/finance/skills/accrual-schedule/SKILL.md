# Accrual Schedule

Per schedule: the originating transaction, the total, the period covered,
the method, and the resulting entry per period through to completion.

Show the full schedule to its end, not only the current period. The value
of a schedule is that somebody can see the tail without rebuilding it.

Reconcile the schedule total against the balance sheet account every period
and report the difference. A schedule that has drifted from the ledger is
worse than none, because it is trusted.

Flag anything ending, anything with an unusual life against the authored
policy, and anything added this period so it can be checked once at the
start rather than discovered later.
