# Controls Test

State the population, its size and source, the sampling method and the
size, before selecting. A sample nobody can reproduce evidences nothing.

Per item: what was tested, what evidence was examined, the result, and the
date tested. An exception records what was expected, what was found, and
the difference.

Classify deficiencies against the authored bands, based on what could go
wrong and how likely, not on whether anything did go wrong this time.

A control that cannot be tested because the evidence was not retained is a
deficiency in itself. Record it as one rather than as untested.
