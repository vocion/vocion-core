# The spine — what a client proposal is made of

Twelve sheets is the shape that has closed. Fewer when a sheet has nothing to
say; never more than one idea per sheet. The buyer is usually one person
carrying this into an internal business case: write for the meeting they will
be in without you.

| # | Sheet | What it does | Components |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cover** | Client logo lockup · eyebrow (`<Offer> · Prepared for <name> · <Month year>`) · h1 promise in the client's terms · accent subtitle · mission paragraph as the lede · `.bdt` rows for **The goal**, **The build**, **The service** · a three-step `.journey` (month 1 → months 2–3 → month 4) · the `fsplit` footer "Prepared by …". | `.cover`, `.hero`, `.eyebrow`, `.lede`, `.bdt`, `.journey` |
| 2 | **What you're buying** | The products side by side as three small windows (`.ovcards`), each with a one-line caption; **Why these, in this order** — three short paragraphs, each a reason the sequence is right; **Measures established at kickoff** as a `.sysrow` of chips; a one-line caption that screens are illustrative. | `.ovcards`, `.drow`, `.sysrow` |
| 3–5 | **One product per sheet** | Numbered h2 (`1.` `2.` `3.`) with a `.pill` eyebrow ("Product 1 · Built first"); a lede in plain words; the client's **pull-quote** with name and date on the sheet their words drove; a step strip or a window mock; two columns — **What changes** / **What does not change** (or **What it reads** / **What it does with it**); an honesty line where the product has a limit. | `.journey` (5 steps), `.win2`/`.plat`, `.mock-cap`, `.twocol`, `.drow` |
| 6 | **Measurement and impact** | The equation the client does not have yet, as `.drow` rows each ending in a `.ph` "to be baselined" where the number is unknown; a categories-only dashboard (`.dtiles` reading Baseline · Measured · Tracked · Target, no numbers); **What Metacto commits to** — the baseline in week 1, the same measures every month, the honest read at the end; the sentence that the seller commits to the capability and to measuring, never to a return figure. | `.drow`, `.ph`, `.dtiles`, `.lede` |
| 7 | **The plan, month by month** | A de-boxed `.gantt` (workstreams × months, solid for build, lighter for live-and-compounding, a `.g-gate` at the decision, a `.g-note` anchoring week 1 to a real kickoff); a `.journey` of what each month ends with; one line on what it is sequenced around (the other project it must not touch). | `.gantt`, `.journey` |
| 8 | **What "<Offer>" means** | The service, not a licence: what the monthly fee covers as `.drow` rows (build · running it · tuning · training and adoption · measurement); **The team** as a capability table — capability and what it owns here, never names or headcount, with the line that capabilities are provided as individual or combined resources; **The rhythm** (weekly working session · monthly measured report · the end-of-term read). | `.drow`, table, `.sysrow` |
| 9 | **Investment** | `.money` block: per-month · term · total; a month-by-month table of what lands and what is invoiced; a line on what is invoiced when and that run costs after handoff are estimated once volumes are known, never bundled; **At the end of <term>, you choose** — three `.offer` columns (take the handoff · move to maintenance · point the team at what's next); the named concession box (a credit earned for a published reference, after approval of every word); the ownership paragraph (client owns its data, records, archive, trained models and custom deliverables; seller keeps pre-existing platform components; matches the MSA). | `.money`, table, `.offer`, `.lede` |
| 10 | **What the client brings · What this does not touch · Next steps** | A short numbered list of what the client supplies and by when (sponsor and owner · one site to start · access within N days · the baseline numbers · the vocabulary · any archive · weekly time); the explicit out-of-scope list; **Next steps** as Questions · Confirm (MSA + SOW follow; this document is never signed) · Send the inputs · Kickoff on a date you set. | `.drow`, `.bdt` |
| 11 | **Questions your team will ask** | `.faq`: ten to twelve questions the buyer's colleagues will raise, each answered in two or three sentences; answers that are yes start with "Yes."; the people question answered flat (no per-person scorecards, because a system people feel watched by is one they work around). | `.faq` |
| 12 | **Appendix · where this goes after** | `.apx` rows grouped by outcome (raise quality further · lower cost to serve · move faster), each marked out of scope; the sentence that nothing here is scheduled, priced or promised, and what comes next is decided at the end-of-term read. | `.apx`, `.futtag` |

## How the spine flexes

- **A scope doc** drops sheets 8 and 11, keeps 6, 7, 9, 10.
- **A partnership update** keeps 1, 6, 7 and the ask; the products become "what changed" and "what's next".
- **A second buyer at the same client** gets a **new dated document**, never a `-v2` of the one the first buyer saw. A reviewed document is immutable; a revision to it is a new version with a new dated title, and the reviewed one stays in the history.

## Where the sheets come from

Nothing on a sheet is invented. Before the first sheet, the room's newest call
is filed with a **numbered decision log** — the decisions and corrections a
person actually said, with the words they used — and each sheet traces back to
a line in it. "Managed AI" is a sheet title because the seller called it that on
the phone; the price is the one he quoted; the three products are the three he
named at the close. When a log line is missing for a claim, the claim is a `.ph`
placeholder and an open item on the room, not a sentence.
