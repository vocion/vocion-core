name: Take a client ask
description: >-
  How a {{company}} client ask is written down so the growth loop can brief it
  without coming back with questions — who asked, what it should move, by when,
  and what the work must not claim.
version: 1
---

Write down four things, in the client's words where you can:

1. **Who asked** — the client and the person, for `requestedBy`.
2. **What it should move** — a number the client watches (sign-ups, demo
   requests, a page's traffic from one channel), never a deliverable. "A blog
   post" is not an ask; "more demo requests from finance teams" is.
3. **By when**, if they said.
4. **What it must not say** — anything the client ruled out: a competitor
   named, a figure they cannot back, a customer they cannot name.

If you cannot write the second line from what the client said, the ask is not
ready: send the account director one question to put to the client. Do not
invent the number.

Then hand the four lines to the demand strategist as the evidence for a brief.
