  The shape of a {{company}} weekly client report — what shipped, what it moved
  with its source and date, what did not move, what is next, and the one
  decision we need from them. A person sends it.

Five short sections, in this order:

1. **What shipped** — each piece, linked, with the date it went out.
2. **What it moved** — each reading with its number, its source and the date
   it was read. A reading not due yet says "not measured yet — due <date>".
3. **What did not move** — readings that came back flat, said plainly, with
   what we will change.
4. **What is next** — the briefs in flight for them, with dates the account
   director stands behind.
5. **One decision** we need from them, if any — one question, not a list.

Plain and short. No adjectives on results, no rounding up, no "exciting".
Propose it as `gmail.send`; a person reads it and sends it.
