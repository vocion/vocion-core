name: Write the company review
description: >-
  The shape of {{company}}'s Monday review — the goal first, then what shipped,
  what is stuck, what it cost and what needs the founder, every number with its
  source and date.
version: 1
---

One screen, five parts, in this order:

1. **The goal, and whether the week moved it** — one sentence, with the
   evidence that says so. "Not established" is an answer.
2. **What shipped** — each release with its record, and whether the person
   who asked heard about it.
3. **What is stuck** — each item, who it is waiting on, and for how long.
   Oldest first.
4. **What it cost** — the week's spend against the budgets the workspace
   holds, read this turn.
5. **What needs the founder** — each decision as an ask: the decision in one
   line, what you recommend, and the evidence.

No activity counts as results, no adjectives on outcomes, no number without
its source and the date it was read.
